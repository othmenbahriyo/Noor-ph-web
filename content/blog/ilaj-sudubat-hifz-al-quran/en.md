## Do not wait for ideal conditions

It has rightly been said: "Walk toward Allah even limping and wounded, for good health does not last forever." Do not wait for perfect conditions to begin your memorization journey; start regardless of your circumstances, your age, or your current level. What matters is consistency, not perfection from day one.

## What helps you persevere in memorization

Whenever motivation wanes, it helps to return to certain hadiths that recall the merit of memorizing the Quran:

- "If the Quran were placed in a skin and thrown into the fire, it would not burn" (narrated by ad-Darimi, from Uqba ibn Amir) — an image that highlights the greatness of the Quran and its preservation by Allah, and by extension that of those who carry it.
- "The best among you is the one who learns the Quran and teaches it" (hadith of Uthman ibn Affan, al-Bukhari).
- "Recite the Quran, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection to intercede for those who used to recite it" (narrated by Muslim, from Abu Umama).
- "The one who recites the Quran with fluency will be with the noble and virtuous angels, and the one who recites it with difficulty, stammering over it, will have a double reward" (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim, from Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her).

This last hadith is especially important for those facing difficulties: the reward is doubled for the one who perseveres despite hardship, not only for the one who memorizes with ease.

## Practical steps to overcome difficulty in memorization

1. **Daily and regular repetition** — the Quran slips quickly from memory, as the hadith reminds us: "Keep refreshing your knowledge of this Quran, for by Him in Whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, it slips away faster than a camel from its tying rope" (narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim). Whoever finds memorization difficult must repeat more, not less.
2. **Listen to an experienced reciter** and repeat the passage with him: listening fixes correct pronunciation even before memorization itself.
3. **Understand the meaning of the verses** before or while memorizing them: understanding greatly facilitates later recall.
4. **Choose a suitable time and place** — memorizing early in the morning, in a calm atmosphere, is more effective than doing so late at night or in a noisy place.
5. **Have someone review with you** — reciting aloud in front of another person reveals mistakes one would not notice alone.
6. **Supplication and seeking forgiveness**, especially before dawn, to ask Allah's help in memorizing and retaining it.

## Overcoming discouragement and slackening

Everyone who has embarked on the path of memorization has gone through a phase of discouragement. To overcome it:

- Remember that discouragement is a passing phase, not the end of the journey.
- Read the biographies of those who memorized the Quran before us — they rekindle motivation.
- Change your place or method of memorization from time to time to break the routine.
- Never stop completely, even on the hardest days — even if it is just a single verse: continuity matters more than quantity.

## The effect of sins on memorization

It is well known among scholars that the multiplication of sins weakens memory and clouds mental clarity. It is reported that Imam ash-Shafi'i complained to his teacher Waki' ibn al-Jarrah about the weakness of his memory; the latter replied to him: "Help yourself memorize by abandoning sins." Ash-Shafi'i then composed these verses:

> I complained to Waki' about the weakness of my memory  
> He guided me to abandon sins  
> And informed me that knowledge is a light  
> And the light of Allah does not guide one who disobeys Him

Whoever desires solid memorization must therefore begin with piety and distance from sin, while multiplying requests for forgiveness.

## Causes of forgetting the Quran after memorizing it

- Lack of regular review.
- Insufficient repetition during the initial memorization.
- Lack of concentration during memorization.
- Absence of understanding of the meaning of what was memorized.

## Valuable advice for those embarking on this path

1. Make your intention sincere for Allah in memorization and review.
2. Adopt a single edition of the Quran and do not change it.
3. Link memorization to understanding the meaning.
4. Mark verses that resemble each other with specific notes to avoid confusing them.
5. Never stop, even if memorization seems difficult — consistency takes priority over speed.
6. Find a companion to review with regularly.
7. Maintain a fixed daily review, even if brief.

## Conclusion

Difficulty in memorization is not a sign of failure, but a stage that everyone who walks this path goes through.
